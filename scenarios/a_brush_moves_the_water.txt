# Paint a ridge across a solved document from outside the process, and watch the water go

# TODO(jb-comment): what makes this reproducible without counting frames — which reply is

observe log

fixed-delta 1/60

new 256 256 7 continents
wait bake
zoom fit

# TODO(jb-comment): why the layer is added before anything is painted, and what an unpainted
# paint layer does to the bake.
layer add height paint
observe layers
observe brush

brush mode add radius 10 falloff 0.4 strength 0.9 value 1
observe brush

# TODO(jb-comment): why the baseline solve comes after the layer is added rather than before.
solve-water
wait water
observe water
observe document
observe field

# TODO(jb-comment): what a ridge laid across the middle of a drained landscape is expected to
# do to it, and why the assertion is the lake count and not the picture.
stroke 16,128 64,120 128,132 192,122 240,130
observe field

# TODO(jb-comment): the claim this line exists to make — `baked` has to still read `whole`,
# because a stroke re-bakes the ground it moved rather than dropping the document's extent.
observe document
capture /tmp/watershed-scenario/painted-ridge.png

solve-water
observe water

# TODO(jb-comment): why cutting the same line back out is the other half of the assertion,
